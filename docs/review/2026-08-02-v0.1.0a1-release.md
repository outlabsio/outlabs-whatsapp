## Post-release consumer hardening

https://github.com/outlabsio/creditos-del-norte-api/pull/36, merged to `main` as
`0a903389c937acbbc65fc96fe378bce337db97cd`. Link-bearing templates mint only after the durable

## Activation boundary

This pre-alpha release and its consumer pins do not enable production Meta delivery. CdN remains
fake-only and rejects production WhatsApp configuration; QDarte remains capture-only with no Meta
client. CdN still requires reviewed Spanish consent/template approval, a real staging-number status
callback, staging operational exercises, and a separate real-PII production approval. QDarte still
requires a tenant-specific production WABA/number, provider implementation, reviewed product
consent/templates, and an operator-approved live rollout. The shared Outlabs test WABA is evidence,
not a production tenant asset.

This post-release record and the changelog date are bookkeeping commits on `main`; they do not alter
the immutable release tag or attached artifacts.
